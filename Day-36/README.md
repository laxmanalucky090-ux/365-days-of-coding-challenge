## LeetCode Problem 125 – Valid Palindrome (Easy)

Today I solved LeetCode Problem 125 – Valid Palindrome.

This problem helped me understand the importance of **string manipulation** and the **two-pointer technique**.

It taught me how to:
- ignore non-alphanumeric characters
- compare characters in a case-insensitive way

### Key learning:
- Convert characters to lowercase for comparison
- Skip non-alphanumeric characters
- Use two pointers from both ends to check palindrome efficiently (**O(n) time**)

Small steps every day lead to big results.  
Consistency matters more than speed.
